A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity, and where it can lead.

I’m David (Chinemerem) Kalu, a student in Nigeria drawn to the overlap between chemistry, mathematics, and computer science.

I’m especially interested in computational chemistry: using code and models to understand how atoms and reactions behave, and what that understanding might make possible. I like questions that move between the theoretical and the practical—from what happens at a molecular scale to how an idea can become a useful tool.

My curiosity has taken me through science and economics competitions, research and engineering programs, student leadership, and creative work. I’m still learning where those paths will lead, and this site is a place to think out loud along the way.

Competition made me curious.

I represented Nigeria at the International Economics Olympiad in 2025 and 2026, and earned a silver medal and national award at the 2026 International Chemistry Competition. I’ve also taken part in mathematics, STEM, and economics competitions.

02 / BUILDING

Ideas are better when tested.

As a PEAR scholar, I worked with a team through Python, hardware integration, research, and problem-solving, then helped build technology solutions for the program’s capstone challenge.

I’m a Global Student Ambassador with Crimson Education, founded a FinFuture Academy chapter, and lead graphic design at International Neural Networks. I also beta-test Dia, The Browser Company’s web browser.

THE LONG VIEW

I have a pretty big dream.

One day, I want to contribute to creating two new chemical elements. It’s an enormous ambition, and I know getting anywhere near it means years of learning, careful research, and working with people who know far more than I do. For now, I’m building the foundations and following the questions that excite me.

At The Ambassadors Schools in Ota, I completed the Honors Program and finished with a 4.19 weighted GPA, ranking first overall twice. Outside class, Model United Nations gave me another way to explore ideas: I served as an Honorary Delegate of Azerbaijan and President of the UNESCO bloc at BIMUN.

OFF THE CLOCK

I watch anime, write, make graphics, and collect the kind of questions that turn into long conversations. Science is a big part of me, but it isn’t the only part.
